can act as both waves and particles. Originally performed with light by Thomas Young in 1801 to

Bright bands form where wave crests meet and amplify each other (constructive interference).
Dark bands form where a crest meets a trough and they cancel each other out (destructive
interference).
This is called an interference pattern.

quantum particles, such as electrons or photons (particles of light).
